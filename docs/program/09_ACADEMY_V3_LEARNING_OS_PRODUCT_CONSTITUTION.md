# TecPey Academy V3 — Learning OS Product Constitution

**Status:** Proposed canonical product and architecture authority  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Scope:** Academy Terms 1–7, Infinite Growth / Term 8, Mentor, Trading Arena learning, League, gamification, assessment, curriculum content, learner-facing UI/UX  
**Branch:** `codex/academy-infinite-growth-v2`  
**Supersedes:** no existing authority by deletion. This document extends and tightens the Infinite Growth V2 contract and Mastery Seasons blueprint. Conflicts must be resolved explicitly before implementation.

---

## 1. Executive decision

TecPey Academy V3 is not a conventional course, content library, quiz app, or points game. It is a **financial learning operating system** designed to build durable, transferable competence and safer independent decision-making.

The system is rebuilt around five coupled layers:

1. Learning Science.
2. Curriculum Architecture.
3. Evidence and Adaptive Engine.
4. Gamification and Learning Economy.
5. Experience and Visual System.

UI polish must never outrun these layers. A beautiful screen built on weak pedagogy, opaque scoring, unsafe incentives, or unverifiable personalization is not an acceptable Academy V3 implementation.

The seven core terms remain the finite certifiable foundation. Term 8 / Infinite Growth remains an ongoing post-core learning workspace and must not be modeled as a finite eighth content bundle.

---

## 2. North-star outcome

TecPey optimizes for a learner who can:

- retrieve important knowledge after time has passed;
- explain a concept in their own reasoning rather than merely recognize an answer;
- distinguish evidence, uncertainty, narrative and speculation;
- apply a concept when surface details change;
- identify unsafe or low-quality decisions;
- size and manage simulated risk with discipline;
- recognize misconceptions and confidence mismatch;
- reflect on errors without shame;
- select an appropriate next action independently;
- know when **not** to trade or act.

Completion, clicks, time-on-page, raw activity, XP, streak length and simulated profit are not substitutes for competence.

## 3. Canonical learning loop

A governed concept should move through the following lifecycle where appropriate:

```text
Discover
  → Understand
  → Retrieve
  → Apply
  → Decide
  → Reflect
  → Repair
  → Reassess
  → Space
  → Transfer
```

This is not a mandatory screen sequence. It is an instructional contract.

### Discover
Create relevance and curiosity without hype, fear, profit promises or artificial urgency.

### Understand
Teach the smallest accurate mental model required for the objective. Progressive disclosure is preferred to unnecessary upfront complexity.

### Retrieve
Require recall, explanation or reconstruction. Recognition-only multiple choice is insufficient as the sole evidence.

### Apply
Use examples with changed surface features so the learner must select and use the concept rather than repeat memorized wording.

### Decide
Present realistic uncertainty and trade-offs. Financial decisions should include a valid no-action/no-trade path where appropriate.

### Reflect
Capture reasoning, confidence and error interpretation. Reflection is metacognitive evidence, never independent mastery authority.

### Repair
Use misconception-specific explanation, worked examples, Socratic support, smaller practice units and targeted retrieval.

### Reassess
Test the repaired concept with materially different items/scenarios.

### Space
Schedule later retrieval using a versioned, observable policy. Avoid punitive notification or streak pressure.

### Transfer
Apply knowledge in a different context, including governed Arena scenarios where relevant.

## 4. Curriculum architecture

Terms are journeys, not folders of lessons. Existing content is not preserved merely because it already exists. Every lesson, question, example and interaction must justify its place against the learning objective.

### Canonical macro progression

The exact names may evolve after curriculum review, but the architecture should cover:

1. **Foundation** — money, crypto primitives, custody, networks, security and uncertainty.
2. **Market Mechanics** — orders, liquidity, spread, volatility, market structure and execution.
3. **Analysis** — evidence, price/volume interpretation, technical/fundamental context, invalidation and limitations.
4. **Risk** — position sizing, expectancy, drawdown, diversification, loss boundaries and scenario risk.
5. **Decision & Psychology** — FOMO, revenge behavior, overconfidence, confirmation bias, process discipline and uncertainty.
6. **Trading Systems** — hypotheses, rules, testing, journaling, execution quality and failure analysis.
7. **Professional Practice** — integrated decisions, security, ethics, risk process, independent research and graduation transfer.
8. **Infinite Growth** — adaptive recurring mastery, repair, market updates, Arena discipline and lifelong retrieval.

Final term naming and lesson topology require a separate curriculum map audit before replacement of current production content.

### Required concept anatomy

Each governed concept/lesson should define, where applicable:

- concept ID and version;
- learning objectives;
- prerequisites;
- safety criticality;
- mental model;
- worked example;
- counter-example;
- common misconceptions;
- evidence/source provenance and freshness where claims can change;
- retrieval items;
- application/transfer scenario;
- reflection prompt where useful;
- remediation path;
- reassessment path;
- spaced follow-up policy;
- FA/EN semantic parity;
- accessibility notes;
- item/content retirement rules.

## 5. Assessment architecture

Assessment exists to collect useful evidence, not to manufacture difficulty.

### Item quality

Every item must be reviewed for:

- objective alignment;
- factual correctness;
- answerability from taught material or declared prerequisites;
- plausible distractors based on real misconceptions;
- explanatory feedback;
- difficulty appropriateness;
- duplicate/near-duplicate detection;
- answer-position bias;
- locale parity;
- source freshness for time-sensitive claims;
- accessibility and unambiguous language.

### Evidence hierarchy

The system distinguishes:

- exposure;
- guided practice;
- retrieval;
- application;
- transfer;
- reassessment;
- reflection.

One correct response, one flashcard, Mentor opinion, self-report, speed or activity cannot independently establish durable mastery.

### Feedback

Feedback should explain:

1. why the selected reasoning is correct or problematic;
2. the underlying mental model;
3. the misconception if identifiable;
4. what evidence would change the conclusion;
5. the next useful learning action.

## 6. Adaptive Evidence Engine

Personalization must be deterministic and replayable for a given governed evidence snapshot and policy version.

Every consequential adaptive decision should be capable of carrying:

- tenant/workspace/student scope;
- concept and item/content identity;
- content/item version;
- evidence type;
- evidence source/authority;
- observed time;
- outcome/strength/confidence where valid;
- diagnosis policy version;
- diagnosis `asOf`;
- evidence snapshot hash;
- stable reason codes.

Missing or stale evidence produces an explicit insufficient-evidence state. The system must not fabricate personalization.

### Explainability contract

For any important personalized recommendation the learner should be able to answer:

- Why did TecPey select this?
- Which kind of evidence contributed?
- Is this a diagnosed weakness, decaying evidence, safety-critical refresher or curriculum-ready recommendation?
- What should I do next?
- What evidence will change after I complete it?

Do not expose pseudo-precise internal scores when they are not calibrated for learner interpretation.

## 7. Infinite Growth / Term 8

Infinite Growth is the recurring adaptive engine after the seven-term foundation.

Canonical bounded cycle:

```text
Evidence
→ Diagnose
→ Explain
→ Worked Example / Socratic Support
→ Practice
→ Retrieval
→ Transfer
→ Reassess
→ Remediate or Advance
→ Schedule Next Retrieval
```

A learner never permanently completes Infinite Growth.

Season types may include:

- personal repair;
- security refresher;
- risk discipline;
- market-context update;
- Arena transfer;
- decision/psychology repair;
- cohort learning challenge;
- advanced mastery.

Market-dependent content must carry named provenance and freshness. AI-generated curriculum remains an untrusted draft until governed review/approval.

---

## 8. Trading Arena as transfer laboratory

Arena is an educational transfer environment, not a profit competition.

Educational evidence may reward:

- position-sizing discipline;
- explicit invalidation reasoning;
- rule compliance;
- journal quality;
- risk-budget adherence;
- recognition of low-quality setups;
- correct no-trade decisions;
- correction of previously observed mistakes.

Simulated P&L can be contextual feedback but must not dominate Academy mastery, XP or League Score. Real-money P&L, wealth, deposits, leverage and trade volume are forbidden learning-rank inputs.

---

## 9. Mentor role

Mentor is a coach, explainer and interface to governed evidence.

Mentor may:

- explain concepts and misconceptions;
- use Socratic questions;
- provide governed worked examples;
- summarize evidence;
- recommend retrieval/remediation;
- explain why a mission was selected;
- help the learner reflect.

Mentor may not independently:

- create mastery;
- change official assessment history;
- create League Score;
- issue credentials;
- grant special/financial opportunities;
- convert self-report into authoritative evidence;
- publish AI-generated official curriculum without governance.

---

## 10. Gamification philosophy

Gamification supports **autonomy, competence, relatedness, safe persistence and meaningful progress**. It must not optimize compulsive engagement.

The desired feeling is not “I must keep tapping.” It is “I can see myself becoming more capable.”

### Prohibited patterns

- casino visual language;
- variable-reward dark patterns;
- loot-box-like mechanics;
- artificial urgency;
- punitive streak loss designed to force low-value activity;
- confetti for routine clicks;
- unlimited grindable XP;
- raw speed rewards without correctness;
- rank rewards for risky trading;
- paid-plan score advantage;
- universal points that silently mix competence, engagement and privilege.

### Celebration hierarchy

Routine actions receive restrained acknowledgement.

Meaningful celebrations are reserved for milestones such as:

- repairing a persistent misconception;
- demonstrating transfer after spaced reassessment;
- completing a demanding governed season;
- improving risk discipline;
- graduating a core term;
- completing the seven-term foundation;
- achieving a governed long-horizon mastery milestone.

Celebration intensity must reflect educational significance.

## 11. Progress model

TecPey must not collapse learning into a single opaque score.

The learner model contains separate visible dimensions where useful:

- Knowledge;
- Recall;
- Application;
- Decision Quality;
- Risk Discipline;
- Consistency.

These dimensions require governed definitions before learner-facing numerical percentages are introduced.

### Progress Core

Progress Core is a **visual progress composition**, not mastery authority.

It should evolve visually through meaningful layers such as:

- core foundation;
- repair stability;
- current-knowledge pulse;
- risk-discipline shield;
- temporary season band;
- advanced mastery layer.

Any numeric Progress Core representation must expose its composition and must state what it does and does not mean. It cannot imply a psychometric competence percentage unless validated for that purpose.

---

## 12. Learning XP

Learning XP is motivational feedback only.

XP policy must be:

- versioned;
- bounded;
- explainable;
- protected by daily/action caps or diminishing returns where farming is possible;
- weighted toward meaningful learning behavior rather than raw activity;
- independent of payment tier;
- unable to create mastery, credential or financial entitlement.

Repeated low-value actions must approach zero marginal XP.

Examples of potentially meaningful XP inputs include governed retrieval, completed remediation, reflection quality signals with appropriate authority limits, and consistency. Exact weights require a separately versioned scoring contract and anti-gaming tests.

---

## 13. Streaks

Streaks represent healthy learning continuity, not obedience.

Required properties:

- grace/recovery mechanism;
- no forced trading or low-quality activity to preserve a streak;
- learner-visible rules;
- timezone-safe calculation;
- server authority;
- accessibility and reduced-motion-safe presentation;
- no shame language after interruption.

A recovery action should be educationally useful, not a paid or arbitrary restore mechanic.

---

## 14. League architecture

League is an opt-in educational competition.

Preferred structure:

- Personal Best / self-improvement;
- comparable cohort competition;
- seasonal learning challenges;
- advanced mastery challenges.

Users at materially different learning stages should not be naively ranked against each other.

Allowed governed dimensions may include:

- assessment accuracy;
- verified improvement;
- remediation completion;
- consistency;
- journal/process quality;
- rule compliance;
- Arena discipline.

Forbidden inputs include:

- real P&L;
- account wealth;
- deposits;
- leverage;
- raw trading volume/frequency;
- raw speed without correctness;
- paid tier;
- engagement spam.

League score must be versioned, auditable, explainable and protected by anti-grinding controls. Ranking visibility requires consent.

League results are recognition. They do not automatically grant cash, funded capital, employment or privileged access. Such programs require a separate governed authority.

## 15. Experience architecture

Academy V3 should feel like a **premium financial learning environment**, not a school LMS, exchange terminal or casual mobile game.

### Experience principles

- calm confidence over visual noise;
- content first;
- strong information hierarchy;
- progressive disclosure;
- clear next action;
- meaningful data visualization;
- motion that communicates state/change;
- restrained glass/transparency;
- premium typography;
- mobile-first without impoverishing desktop;
- FA RTL and EN LTR designed as first-class experiences;
- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum;
- visible keyboard focus;
- 44px preferred primary touch targets;
- 200% zoom resilience;
- reduced motion/transparency support;
- accessible loading/error/empty/degraded states.

### Six questions every important screen must answer

1. Where am I?
2. What am I learning or repairing?
3. Why is this here for me?
4. What evidence changed?
5. What should I do next?
6. What does this progress/score mean, and what does it **not** mean?

---

## 16. Mission experience

A mission is a coherent learning experience, not a checklist of widgets.

A mission may include:

1. meaningful hook;
2. concise mental model/refresher;
3. retrieval;
4. misconception check;
5. worked example;
6. scenario decision;
7. Arena transfer when relevant;
8. Mentor feedback;
9. reflection;
10. reassessment;
11. scheduled future retrieval.

Timers are prohibited for first exposure. They may be used only where time pressure is itself part of the validated learning objective.

---

## 17. Content quality and scientific integrity

Content must be scientifically and practically useful rather than long for its own sake.

For every curriculum rebuild:

- identify learning objective first;
- separate durable principles from time-sensitive market claims;
- cite authoritative/current sources for mutable claims;
- label uncertainty;
- avoid price predictions and profit promises;
- use realistic examples and counterexamples;
- teach limitations of tools/indicators;
- teach security and risk before advanced execution;
- include no-trade/no-action reasoning;
- include common failure modes;
- test transfer, not just recall;
- maintain content provenance/version history.

The system should teach learners to challenge claims—including TecPey's own explanations—using evidence and reasoning.

---

## 18. Measurement

Product success must not be reduced to DAU, streaks or lesson completion.

Learning/product metrics should include, where statistically valid:

- delayed retrieval performance;
- reassessment improvement;
- transfer performance;
- misconception repair rate;
- calibration/confidence mismatch;
- remediation effectiveness;
- Arena rule-discipline improvement;
- voluntary return to useful learning;
- learner comprehension of recommendation reasons;
- accessibility/task-completion quality.

Engagement metrics remain operational signals, not proof of learning.

Small samples must be labeled insufficient rather than converted into unstable analytics.

---

## 19. Anti-gaming and fairness

- Server-backed authority for consequential progress/rank.
- Idempotency/replay protection for mutations.
- Duplicate low-value evidence cannot create unlimited reward.
- Policy versions remain attached to historical decisions.
- Client state cannot create mastery/rank/credential.
- FA/EN objectives and scoring remain semantically equivalent.
- Paid tier cannot improve official learning rank.
- Cohorts must avoid misleading comparisons.
- Appeals/integrity holds fail closed where ranking authority requires them.
- Personalization must degrade to explicit insufficient evidence.

## 20. Architecture implementation order

Academy V3 is implemented in this order:

### Phase 0 — Preserve authority
Record this constitution, existing contracts, decision history and acceptance criteria in-repository before major redesign.

### Phase 1 — Curriculum map
Audit Terms 1–7 at concept/objective level. Produce the canonical concept graph, prerequisite graph, safety-critical map, item inventory and FA/EN parity map.

### Phase 2 — Assessment and content system
Rebuild item quality, provenance/versioning, misconception model, feedback, transfer scenarios and content-source governance.

### Phase 3 — Evidence engine
Complete governed evidence ingestion, diagnosis, freshness, replay identity, remediation and reassessment authority.

### Phase 4 — Learning economy
Version XP, streak, Progress Core and League policies with anti-grinding/fairness tests.

### Phase 5 — Experience system
Create the Academy V3 information architecture, design tokens/components, mission UX, term journey, progress visualization, celebrations and accessibility behavior.

### Phase 6 — Arena and Mentor orchestration
Connect transfer missions and coaching without authority leakage.

### Phase 7 — Infinite Growth
Make Term 8 a fully adaptive recurring learning workspace using the same concept/evidence architecture.

### Phase 8 — Validation
Strict FA/EN content QA, accessibility, mobile/desktop, browser, security, tenant isolation, exact-head CI, governed test-account E2E and staged evidence.

No phase is considered complete because its UI looks finished.

## 21. Decision registry

The following decisions are intentionally preserved as project authority unless superseded by a later explicit ADR/contract:

- Seven core terms remain finite and certifiable.
- Infinite Growth is ongoing and post-core.
- Learning XP, Mastery Evidence, League Score, Credential and Special Opportunity remain separate authorities.
- Mentor is not mastery/rank/credential authority.
- Arena is a transfer lab, not a profit leaderboard.
- Real P&L, wealth, deposits, leverage, raw volume and paid tier cannot improve learning rank/mastery.
- Gamification optimizes competence and persistence, not compulsion.
- Streaks require grace/recovery.
- Progress Core is visualization, not an opaque mastery percentage.
- League is opt-in and cohort-aware.
- Curriculum content can be replaced when it fails the new quality bar.
- Every personalized recommendation must be explainable.
- Historical evidence preserves content/item/policy identity.
- AI-generated curriculum is untrusted until governed review.
- Accessibility, FA/EN parity and reduced-motion behavior are architecture requirements, not polish.
- No automatic financial or special entitlement arises from Academy/League rank.

## 22. Definition of Done for Academy V3

Academy V3 is not complete until evidence demonstrates:

- Terms 1–7 have a reviewed concept/prerequisite map and high-quality content architecture;
- every governed lesson has explicit objectives and concept identity;
- assessments test retrieval/application/transfer and provide explanatory feedback;
- item provenance/versioning protects historical evidence;
- personalization is replayable, reason-coded and fails safely;
- remediation and reassessment are real product loops;
- spacing policy is versioned and observable;
- XP cannot be farmed through low-value repetition;
- streak recovery is non-punitive;
- Progress Core is explainable;
- League excludes unsafe/financial/paid inputs and has anti-grinding tests;
- Arena evaluates process/risk discipline more than outcome;
- Mentor cannot leak authority;
- Term 8 continuously adapts and never becomes permanently completed;
- UI answers the six learner questions;
- FA/EN parity, RTL/LTR, WCAG, reduced motion and responsive behavior pass;
- security/tenant/idempotency/replay tests pass;
- exact-head CI and browser golden paths pass;
- staging validation is separately authorized and evidenced.

## 23. Change-control rule

This document exists so Academy V3 decisions survive conversation limits, personnel changes and future implementation waves.

A future implementation may improve or supersede a decision, but must not silently contradict this constitution. Material changes to learning authority, scoring, League, gamification, curriculum topology, Infinite Growth or financial/special entitlements require an explicit documented decision with rationale, migration impact and tests.

**No merge, Staging deployment or Production deployment is authorized by this document.**
