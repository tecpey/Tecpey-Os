# TecPey Academy Monthly Mastery League — Recognition Policy v2

This policy defines the deterministic scoring and recognition contract for
the post-Term-7 infinite growth path. League rank is educational recognition
only. It cannot create cash, capital, a funded-account implication, Arena Pro,
employment access or any other privileged entitlement.

## Monthly score

All inputs and outputs use integer basis points from 0 to 10,000. No floating
point arithmetic is permitted.

| Component | Weight | Authoritative evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Assessment accuracy | 25% | Finalized server-side quizzes and scenario assessments |
| Mastery improvement | 20% | Improvement against the learner's prior committed baseline |
| Learning consistency | 15% | Distinct active learning days; no raw-speed bonus |
| Journal quality | 15% | Governed journal/reflection evidence, not free-form Mentor sentiment |
| Rule compliance | 15% | Finalized virtual Arena risk and discipline evidence |
| Repair completion | 10% | Completed assigned repair missions and verified reassessment |

Forbidden inputs include real or virtual PnL, return, capital, deposit, trade volume,
leverage, paid plan, social popularity, geography, device/network quality
and raw completion speed.

## Eligibility

- explicit scoring and public-ranking consent;
- all seven core terms completed;
- no integrity or appeal hold;
- at least four eligible assessments;
- at least eight active learning days;
- at least six finalized virtual Arena sessions;
- at least two Mentor-reviewed reflections;
- same tenant, workspace, locale, mastery level and calendar-month cohort;
- public cohort suppressed below 25 eligible learners.

## Bands and ranking

- Diamond: 9,000–10,000
- Gold: 8,000–8,999
- Silver: 7,000–7,999
- Bronze: 6,000–6,999
- Foundation: below 6,000

Ranking is dense: equal scores share the same rank. A future public snapshot must
use a secret-backed stable display key for ordering inside ties; enrollment time,
name, wealth, speed and randomness are forbidden tie-breakers.

## Recognition and opportunity boundary

- Rank is recognition only for every place, including the top 10.
- `arenaProDays` and `cashPoolShareBps` remain zero for every league result.
- The automatic entitlement drain is disabled and must not query or write grants.
- Existing grants remain readable until a separately approved migration changes them.
- A formal opportunity requires separate enrollment, specialist evaluation,
  recorded approval and an auditable authority. League position is never sufficient.
- Ties use dense rank; they do not divide or create anything of financial value.

## Infinite Term 8

Term 8 is the learner-facing name for ongoing Mastery Seasons after the seven
certifiable core terms. Every generated mission is an untrusted draft until it
passes source freshness, educational quality, safety, question validity,
personalization, bias sampling and governed publication review. Mentor AI may
draft and recommend; it cannot certify, rank, reward or publish by itself.
