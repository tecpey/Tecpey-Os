# TecPey Learning OS — enterprise QA baseline

Date: 2026-08-31  
Scope: user dashboard, profile, Academy, Trading Arena, league, scoring, credentials and orchestration.

## Release decision

Staging promotion is paused until this baseline is accepted by CI and review. Production is out of scope.

## Canonical learning architecture

| Channel | Purpose | Public default | Competitive | Automatic financial or privileged entitlement |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Learning XP | Engagement and progress feedback | No | No | Never |
| Mastery evidence | Verified competence and repair | No | No | Never |
| League score | Opt-in seasonal comparison | No | Yes | Never |
| Credential | Verifiable achievement with criteria and evidence | No | No | Never |
| Special opportunity | Separate formal-program workflow | No | No | Never automatic |

Score channels must not be added together or relabeled as one universal “points” balance.

## Term 8 — Infinite Growth

Term 8 is not an eighth finite content bundle. It is a governed post-core workspace that opens only after all seven core terms are passed.

Lifecycle:

1. Assess current mastery and risk discipline.
2. Plan the smallest evidence-backed objective.
3. Practice in Academy or simulated Trading Arena.
4. Reflect in a private learning journal.
5. Verify against explicit criteria.
6. Adapt the next cycle.

Repair recommendations may be shown earlier, but the formal Term 8 workspace remains locked until core graduation.

## League and Arena safety rules

- Explicit scoring consent and separate public-ranking consent.
- Minimum cohort suppression, integrity hold and appeal window.
- No real PnL, deposits, trade volume, leverage, wealth or paid tier in score inputs.
- Simulated outcomes may be bounded evidence, but process quality and rule compliance dominate.
- Rank is recognition only.
- Free-path rank must not create cash, capital, funded-account language, paid-tier access or employment opportunity.
- Formal opportunities require separate program enrollment, specialist evaluation, recorded approval and an auditable authority.

## Observed blockers

1. Term 8 existed as a public Mastery Seasons explainer and read-only recommendation surface, not a protected term.
2. Student dashboard was hard-coded to seven terms and had no post-core next action.
3. Monthly league policy proposed automatic Arena Pro days and cash-pool shares.
4. Authenticated dashboard, profile, Arena and league visual QA requires a governed test account; public routes correctly redirect to Academy login.
5. Public Academy copy must be reviewed for reward, career and simulated-capital implications before staging.

## Acceptance gate

- Unit, type, build, migration and tenant-isolation suites are green.
- Term 8 is inaccessible before Term 7 is passed.
- A seven-term graduate sees Term 8 as the next action without changing core completion from 7/7.
- League results produce recognition only.
- No automatic entitlement job writes new league-derived grants.
- Existing grants remain readable; migration or revocation requires a separate approved change.
- Mobile RTL/LTR, keyboard focus, 200% zoom, reduced motion, empty/error/loading states and authenticated end-to-end flows pass on staging.
- Support form persistence and Living Mentor Workspace are re-verified after deployment.
